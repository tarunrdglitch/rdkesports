## 1. Product Concept & Vision

RDK Esports is a production-grade multi-tenant esports tournament operating system designed for creators, YouTubers, esports organizations, gaming clans, and colleges.
Instead of relying on third-party forms and spreadsheets, organizers control their entire tournament lifecycle:
- Tournament creation with multi-format support
- Direct manual UPI QR code payments without gateway cuts
- Ambassador-driven team & payment verification (managed strictly by Official Creators)
- Live IPL-style player auctions with ephemeral, auto-destruct credentials for bidders
- Automated round management, room ID/pass dispatch, and live score leaderboards
- Google Sheets two-way synchronization

## 2. Platform Roles & Station Hierarchy

1. **👑 Project Head / Super Admin (`super_admin`)**
   - Head owner of the entire project (RDK Technologies).
   - Authorizes, verifies, and onboards Official Creators / Partners.
   - Global platform oversight, monetization settings, and immutable audit logs.
2. **⭐ Official Creator / Partner (`org_owner`)**
   - Verified content creators / tournament organizers created or approved by the Head.
   - Full tenant authority: manages tournaments, teams, ambassador staff, and live auctions.
   - **Exclusive Creator of Ambassadors**: Only Official Creators can create and provision ambassador logins.
   - **Auction Credential Generator**: Creates unique team credentials for live player auctions.
   - Featured on the public landing page with custom avatar, bio, follower count, and social IDs (YouTube, Instagram, Discord).
3. **📋 Ambassador (`ambassador`)**
   - Field staff provisioned **strictly by Official Creators** (no public registration).
   - Assigned to specific tournament team batches (e.g., Teams 1–15).
   - Validates UTR numbers, reviews payment screenshots, verifies player eligibility.
4. **🎮 Team Captain / Auction Bidder (`team_captain`)**
   - Registered competitive user who creates squads, invites players, and submits UPI payment proofs.
   - Can receive temporary, unique auction credentials generated by the Creator for live bidding.
5. **🎯 Player / Audience (`player` / `public`)**
   - Self-registers on the platform, explores public portfolio & live tournaments, accepts team invites, and views match rooms & leaderboards.

## 3. Strict Ambassador & Auction Lifecycle Rules (New)

### A. Creator-Only Ambassador Provisioning
- Normal public registration allows only `player` and `team_captain` accounts.
- Ambassadors **CANNOT** self-register.
- Ambassadors are created exclusively within the Official Creator Portal (`/creator/ambassadors`):
  - Creator sets Ambassador Name, Assigned Tournament, and Team Range (e.g. Teams 1–10).
  - System issues a secure, non-public login.
  - Creator can revoke or reassign ambassadors at any moment.

### B. Ephemeral Auction Credentials (Auto-Deleted Post-Auction)
- When an Official Creator begins or schedules an Auction Tournament:
  - Creator defines the participating teams (e.g., 8–12 teams).
  - Creator generates **Unique Scoped Bidding Credentials** (Team Code + Temporary Access Passkey) for each team captain.
  - Captains log in with their unique auction credentials to access the live bidding room with their pre-loaded team purse.
- **Automatic Destruction Post-Auction**:
  - Once the Creator / Auction Conductor finalizes the auction, **all temporary auction accounts are permanently purged/deleted from the database**.
  - All active session tokens for these bidders are invalidated instantly.
  - Prevents credential reuse or unauthorized platform access after the event concludes.
